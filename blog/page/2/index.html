Webhook retries and idempotency keys

2026-09-18 · guides

Failed webhook deliveries are retried 6 times over 24 hours with exponential backoff. Use the idempotency key in each payload to drop duplicates on your side.

2026-09-02 · release

Batch exports let you pull up to 50K events per job as Parquet or CSV. Jobs run in the background and post a signed download link to your webhook when they finish.
